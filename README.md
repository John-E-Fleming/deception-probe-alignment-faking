# deception-probe-alignment-faking

Testing whether linear probes trained on instructed deception (Apollo Research's
roleplaying, insider trading, sandbagging scenarios) generalise to naturally-emerging
alignment faking. Part 4 of a research series on probe-based deception detection in LLMs.

## Status

Week 1 of a 4-week sprint. In active development — interface and results subject to
change.
